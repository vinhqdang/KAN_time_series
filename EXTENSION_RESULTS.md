**Finding.** A naive last-value forecast is at least as good as every learned model on both data sets (0.0177 vs 0.0245 best deep model on finance). The published comparison omitted this baseline. SPADE-fast (residual + group-lasso, cached bases, 300 epochs) matches the naive forecast and is the best learned model on both, and trains in seconds instead of minutes, but it does not beat naive.
