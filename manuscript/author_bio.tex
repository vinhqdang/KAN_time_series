\begin{center}\Large\textbf{Author Biographies}\end{center}
\vspace{1em}

\textbf{Quang-Vinh Dang} holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Universit\'e de
Lorraine, France, and is a Data and AI leader with over fifteen years of
experience building and scaling data-science teams across fintech, digital
banking, technology platforms, and academia. He currently serves as a Data/AI
Director in industry and as a Lecturer at British University Vietnam, where he
focuses on translating advanced AI research into real-world business impact. He
has led multidisciplinary teams delivering large-scale machine-learning systems
for credit scoring, fraud detection, recommendation, pricing optimization, and
natural language processing, and has published more than forty peer-reviewed
research papers. His interests include interpretable and causal machine learning,
data mining, and responsible AI for data-driven decision-making. He is the
corresponding author of this work and led the design, implementation, and
evaluation of the SPADE method.

\textbf{Dat Le} is a researcher at Ho Chi Minh City University of Economics and
Finance, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam, working at the intersection of artificial
intelligence, financial markets, and geospatial analytics. His research develops
hybrid models that integrate Graph Neural Networks, Kolmogorov--Arnold Networks,
and deep-learning architectures for anomaly detection, trend forecasting, and
digital-twin systems for real-estate markets, combining price, trading-volume,
market-sentiment, and geospatial data to uncover latent dynamics across countries
and market contexts. As a researcher and lecturer he contributes to research and
teaching in machine learning, Bayesian modelling, and data-driven
decision-making, has published in international journals on anomaly-aware
forecasting and spatio-temporal modelling for real-estate and commodity markets,
and collaborates with industry and institutional partners in Australia, Vietnam,
and the United Kingdom to translate theoretical advances into practical analytics
for investors, policymakers, and technology-driven enterprises.

\textbf{Minh Ngoc Dinh} is Dean of Data Science and Information Technology at
Millennia Education, Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer
Science from Monash University (Doctoral Medal, Faculty of Information Technology)
and has taught and conducted research at Monash University, The University of
Queensland, and RMIT University Vietnam, where he served as Deputy Dean (Research
\& Innovation) and as Program Manager for the Master of Artificial Intelligence.
His expertise spans high-performance computing and artificial intelligence, with a
strong record in research and development, academic leadership, and project
management.
