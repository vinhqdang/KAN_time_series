
\section{Experimental Results}

We evaluate CD-KAN comprehensively across synthetic datasets (where ground-truth causal graphs are known) and real-world financial data (where we report stability-based indirect validation, as no ground truth exists). All experiments use strict rolling-origin temporal splits; normalization statistics are computed on training windows only to prevent leakage \citep{Kim2022}.

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Datasets and Experimental Setup}
% ------------------------------------------------------------------

\subsubsection{Datasets}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Synthetic Linear (2 datasets)}: Generated via Vector Autoregression (VAR) with random sparse adjacency matrices ($N \in \{500, 2000\}$, nodes$=5$).
    \item \textbf{Synthetic Nonlinear (2 datasets)}: Structural Causal Models (SCMs) with non-linear functions (sine, tanh) and additive non-Gaussian noise ($N \in \{500, 2000\}$, nodes$=5$).
    \item \textbf{Financial (8 Assets, $N=1\,827$)}: Daily closing prices of Gold (GLD), WTI Crude Oil, U.S. Dollar Index (DXY), S\&P 500 (SPX), Bitcoin (BTC), Silver (SLV), Copper (COPPER), and Natural Gas (NG) from 2020--2025.
    \item \textbf{Cryptocurrency (5 Coins, $N=1\,095$)}: Top-5 cryptocurrencies by market capitalisation.
    \item \textbf{Macroeconomic (5 Series, $N=1\,257$)}: Key indicators including CPI, interest rates, and industrial production.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Baselines}
We compare against seven baselines spanning linear, constraint-based, and score-based methods:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{VAR-Lasso}: Linear VAR with $\ell_1$ regularization \citep{Tibshirani1996}.
    \item \textbf{PCMCI}: Constraint-based method designed for time series \citep{Runge2019}.
    \item \textbf{NTiCD}: Neural time-invariant causal discovery.
    \item \textbf{GOLEM}: Differentiable score-based DAG learning \citep{Ng2020}.
    \item \textbf{NOTEARS} / \textbf{DYNOTEARS}: Continuous optimization for structure learning \citep{Zheng2018,Pamfil2020}.
    \item \textbf{GC-KAN}: KAN-based neural Granger causality \citep{GCKAN2024}.
    \item \textbf{VarLiNGAM}: Linear non-Gaussian acyclic model for time series \citep{Hyvarinen2010}.
\end{enumerate}
Forecasting comparisons additionally include TSMixer, PatchTST, N-BEATS, and LSTM.

Figure~\ref{fig:real_lag_heatmap} displays the expected propagation lag (in trading days) for each significant edge. \subsubsection{Rolling-Time Stability}

To validate that discovered edges reflect genuine structural relationships rather than noise, Figure~\ref{fig:real_stability} reports pairwise Jaccard similarity of the binarized causal graph across three rolling time windows (2020--2024, 2021--2024, 2022--2025). A mean Jaccard of 0.242 reflects moderate stability, consistent with documented macro-financial regime shifts during the 2021--2023 period (pandemic recovery, rate-hike cycle, FTX collapse).

\subsection{Ablation Study}
% ------------------------------------------------------------------

Table~\ref{tab:ablation} quantifies the contribution of each core design choice.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Ablation study on the Financial dataset ($N=1\,827$). Each row removes one component from the full CD-KAN model.}
\label{tab:ablation}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\hline
\textbf{Configuration} & \textbf{Forecasting MSE} $\downarrow$ & \textbf{F1 (Synthetic)} $\uparrow$ \\ \hline
CD-KAN (Full)                     & \textbf{0.0179} & \textbf{0.897} \\
w/o ALM (soft penalty only)        & 0.0214 & 0.718 \\
w/o Lag-Aware Adjacency            & 0.0231 & 0.741 \\
w/o RevIN (fixed normalization)    & 0.0415 & 0.881 \\
w/o KAN (MLP backbone)             & 0.0267 & 0.581 \\
w/o Temperature Annealing          & 0.0198 & 0.842 \\ \hline
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

The largest degradation occurs when removing RevIN ($+$132\% MSE), confirming the importance of per-window normalization for non-stationary financial data. Replacing the NOTEARS-ALM constraint with a soft penalty collapses F1 by 17.9 percentage points, validating the acyclicity fix. Removing the lag-aware adjacency module ($-$Lag-Aware) reduces F1 by 15.6 pp, demonstrating that separating lag-specific edges is critical for identifying temporal causal structure.
