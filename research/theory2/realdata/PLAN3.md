Why the chambers address the CausalRivers failure: the sources of the summary graph are **separate source SCCs by construction** (independent random number streams), so Cor 1.10's condition for a non-trivial value holds by design rather than by assumption, and the slow physical drivers that remain are either measured (atmospheric pressure) or removed by the differencing in the primary preprocessing.

## Theory link and its assumptions (fixed now)

- Population statement used: THEORY.md Prop 2.8 / Cor 2.9: for a stationary process whose summary graph has independent source innovations, non-relatives have zero correlation at every lag, and relatives have non-zero correlation generically. Prop 2.8's zero half uses only that X_i is a function of the innovations of anc_S(i). It therefore survives any per-node time-invariant filter (block means, differencing, subsampling) and any nonlinearity; the non-zero half is a genericity (faithfulness) assumption, which nonlinear mechanisms can violate (see LT caveat below).
- Predicted value: THEORY.md Section 6 rule, unchanged: for an observed node set S, ρ(v) = #{w ∈ S : anc_full(v) ∩ anc_full(w) ≠ ∅} in the full graph of the experiment's random variables (unobserved nodes latent); s̃ = mean over induced non-self-loop edges of 1 / ½ / 0 for ρ(u) <, =, > ρ(v). Graph: `cc_graph.py` (edges copied from causalchamber 0.2.8; constant parameters dropped).
- Stationarity: the WT actuators are mirrored random walks with maximal step uniform in [resolution, range/50]; their mixing time is of the order of the run length, so **levels are not stationary within a run**. The primary preprocessing therefore uses increments of block means (a per-node linear filter, so the theory's zero pattern is preserved). Levels are analysed only as a secondary analysis (S1), with the prediction that they fail.
- Faithfulness caveat, LT: θ_1, θ_2 act on ir_3, vis_3 through Malus' law cos²(θ_1 − θ_2) with θ uniform on [−90, 90]; the linear correlation of θ_k with ir_3, vis_3 is then near zero by symmetry. Prediction: these four relative pairs are usually not detected. This lowers ρ̂(pol_k) and does not change the predicted orientation of any edge.

**LT (replication).** File `actuators_white`, 20,000 rows, split into 10 consecutive segments of 2,000 rows (the units). A node whose preprocessed series is constant in a unit is removed from that unit (reported).

## Estimator (FINITE.md §7 primary test, unchanged in form)

- Pearson r̂_uv of the preprocessed series of a unit.
- n_eff,uv = n / max(1, 1 + 2 Σ_{h=1}^{K} r̂_u(h) r̂_v(h)), with r̂_u(h) the sample autocorrelation of the preprocessed series. K = 20 (WT primary, LT), K = 100 (WT levels S1).
- Declared dependent iff |atanh r̂_uv|·√(n_eff,uv − 3 − k) > z_c, z_c = Φ^{-1}(1 − α/(2m)), α = 0.01, m = d(d−1)/2 pairs of the node set analysed, k = number of conditioning variables (0 except S4).
- ρ̂(v) = 1 + #declared partners in the node set; edge value 1 / ½ / 0 for ρ̂(u) <, =, > ρ̂(v); accuracy = mean edge value.
- Secondary test (S2): the same with nominal n' = n.

## Pre-registered predictions and thresholds (WT primary)

- **P7 (no saturation).** Pooled over the 15 runs (full 16-node set, 63 non-relative pairs per run), the fraction of non-relative pairs declared dependent is **< 0.5**. Cluster-bootstrap 95% CI over runs reported (2,000 resamples, seed 0).
- **P8 (orientation).** Accuracy over the 25 full-graph edges with predicted value 1, pooled over runs (375 edge instances), is **> 0.5 with cluster-bootstrap 95% CI lower bound > 0.5**. The predicted-½ edge pot_1 → signal_1 is reported separately (prediction: tie, value ½).
- **P9 (graded agreement).** Subgraphs: for each run k, 10 node sets of size 5 and 10 of size 8, drawn by `cc_graph.sample_subgraphs` with seed 10·(9000 + k) + d (uniform among sets with ≥ 1 induced edge); 300 subgraphs. The estimator is applied to the subgraph's nodes only (Bonferroni over its pairs). **Spearman(s̃, estimated accuracy) ≥ 0.3.** p-value and a cluster-bootstrap CI over runs reported.
- **Kill rule.** If P7 fails, the data are reported as saturated and P8, P9 are reported but not interpreted as support. If P7 passes and P8 fails, the real-data link of Thm 1.9 is reported as unsupported even on a system with designed independent sources.
- **What counts as a positive real-data demonstration:** P7, P8 and P9 all pass on WT. Nothing else (not the LT replication, not a secondary analysis) is reported as one.

**LT replication (R7, R8, R9).** The same three tests and thresholds on the LT units (10 segments; 126 non-relative pairs per unit; 37 edges with predicted value 1, 370 instances; subgraph seeds 10·(9100 + j) + d for segment j = 0…9, 200 subgraphs). Reported separately; they do not change the WT verdicts.

## Secondary analyses (reported, no verdict)

- **S1** WT levels (no block means, no differencing, n ≈ 10,000), K = 100. Prediction: many independent-actuator pairs are declared dependent (spurious correlation of persistent random walks; the Bartlett correction with finite K under-corrects), so the non-relative fraction is higher than in the primary analysis.
- **S2** nominal-n test on the primary preprocessing (WT and LT). FINITE Cor 1.8 predicts a higher false-positive rate whenever the products of autocorrelations are positive.
- **S3** WT with D = 1 and D = 30 (same steps otherwise). D = 1 may lose relatives whose response is slower than one step (THEORY.md caveat to Prop 2.8, FINITE Rem 1.10).
- **S4** measured-driver conditioning (F3 with a measured driver): WT partial correlations given pressure_ambient (node removed; 15 nodes, 48 non-relative pairs), on the S1 levels and on the primary increments, k = 1, n_eff from the residual ACFs. Reported: non-relative fraction among the barometer pairs and overall, before and after.

## F-theory checks (fixed now; out of sample within each unit)

Each unit's preprocessed series is split into first and second halves. From the first half: r̂_uv and the ACFs, giving n̂_eff for the second half (second-half n with first-half ACF products). Predicted probability that the second-half test declares the pair: p_uv = Φ̄(z_c − |atanh r̂_uv|√(n̂_eff − 3)) + Φ(−z_c − |atanh r̂_uv|√(n̂_eff − 3)) (Fisher-z / Prop 1.7 asymptotics). Reported against the second-half test:
1. F2: mean p over non-relative pairs vs the observed second-half non-relative fraction; median first-half |r̂| over non-relative pairs (κ̂, the implied loading ℓ̂ = κ̂ for equal shares) and n_1/2 = 3 + (z_c/atanh κ̂)² against n_eff.
2. F1: mean p over relative pairs vs the observed second-half recovery rate; first-half ρ̂_min.
3. Predicted orientation accuracy: Monte Carlo of independent Bernoulli(p_uv) decisions (2,000 draws, seed 1) → predicted accuracy on the P8 edges, against the observed second-half accuracy.
4. S4: triad reliability r̂ = median of r̂_uZ r̂_vZ / r̂_uv over non-relative pairs with |r̂_uv| ≥ 0.1 (FINITE Prop 3.8), with Z = pressure_ambient, if any such pairs exist.

No threshold, unit, preprocessing choice, K, D, seed or node set changes after results are seen. Any deviation will be listed in `README.md`.
