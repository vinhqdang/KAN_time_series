# Adaptive group lasso (SSA) at d=500 and sensitivity to its constants (gamma, eps)

Committed BEFORE any run in this directory. Code: copies of research/replication/{bench,basil,basil_s,docalc,spire}.py (timing fix: cost columns exclude metric/do-effect code); bench.py adds only (i) SSA with parameters `SSA_g{gamma}_e{eps}` (bare `SSA` = gamma 1, eps 0.01, identical to research/adaptive), (ii) env NODO=1 to skip do-effect evaluation in the grid. One CPU core (OMP_NUM_THREADS=1, sequential). Nothing under research/ other than research/adaptive2/ is modified.

SSA = SPADE-scaled first fit (screen K=15, lambda=0.2/d), weights w_ij=(||theta_ij||+eps)^-gamma normalised to mean 1 over screened edges, refit from scratch with the same lambda and mask.

## Part A: d=500
Data: iSCM, d=500, n=1000, uniform noise, mech m1, seeds 1000 and 1001 (the seeds used in research/spire/README_do.md). Methods run here in one process per dataset (shared SPADE-scaled first fit): SSC, SSR_k1.5, SSA (gamma 1, eps 0.01), and BASILWs on seed 1000 (seed 1001 BASILWs already exists in research/spire/results/do_stab/; seed 1000 had only pre-stabilisation BASIL-W). Existing SSC/SSR/BASIL-W numbers (research/spire/results/do/d500_*.csv) are shown next to the re-run ones; the SSC/SSR re-run also gives clean timings. Metrics: edge NRMSE, slope, do-effect NRMSE (direct/indirect), AUROC, orientation. Time = fit time only (t_ss + refit; for BASIL-W t_ss + t_mcmc + t_extract), from the fixed timing columns; old `time` columns include do-effect code and are not used.
Predictions (not promises): P1 SSA NRMSE at d=500 about 0.22-0.30 with slope 0.85-0.95, next to SSR (0.23) and worse than BASIL-W (0.13-0.16), as at d=100/200 (gap +0.055 to +0.07); P2 AUROC equals SSC (within 0.005); P3 SSA do-effect NRMSE between SSR and BASIL-W-stabilised within a few hundredths; P4 fit time about t_ss + a refit of the same order as SSR's. SSA may also beat the expectation or fail at d=500 (weights from a heavily shrunken first fit); either outcome will be reported.

## Part B: sensitivity grid
Data: d=100, n=1000, uniform, m1, seeds 2000-2004 (the first 5 replication seeds; BASIL-W mean NRMSE on these 5 = 0.159, SSC 0.610, SSR 0.216, from research/replication/results/all.csv). Per dataset: one SPADE-scaled first fit reused by 9 refits, gamma in {0.5,1,2} x eps in {0.001,0.01,0.1}. Report NRMSE, slope, AUROC per setting (mean over 5 datasets, per-dataset values in the CSVs), and whether any setting reaches BASIL-W's NRMSE.
Decision rule (fixed now): the default (1, 0.01) remains THE SSA configuration for any headline number. The grid is sensitivity analysis only. No setting is selected from these seeds. If another setting is clearly better (mean NRMSE lower by more than 0.03 and better on at least 4 of 5 datasets) this is stated and the recommendation is to validate it on fresh seeds, not to adopt it. A setting "reaches BASIL-W" if its mean NRMSE <= 0.159 + 0.01.
Predictions: Q1 NRMSE varies little across the grid (range < 0.05) around the default's about 0.24; Q2 large eps (0.1) with small gamma (0.5) behaves closest to plain group lasso (higher NRMSE, lower slope); small eps with gamma 2 is the most aggressive reweighting and risks dropping true weak edges (AUROC down); Q3 no setting reaches BASIL-W's NRMSE. Any of these can fail.

## Limitations
2 datasets at d=500 (no significance claims, seeds shared with earlier tables); 5 datasets for the grid (no significance claims; smallest exact Wilcoxon p = 0.0625); one generator family and noise type; SSR/BASIL-W comparison numbers partly from earlier independent runs (same generator and seeds); grid seeds are a subset of seeds on which SSA at the default was already run (research/adaptive), so the grid cannot be used for selection; timings are wall-clock on a shared machine and noisy.
